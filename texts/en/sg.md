## view
This song begins where they got bored with the machines.

Released on 14 January 2002, it reached No. 8 in the UK.{{ref:wp-sg}}{{ref:wp-disc}} Ed later said "Star Guitar" came about when they got bored with the machines. The shifting sound is Tom playing a Parker MIDI Fly guitar, sent through an effect into the computer.{{ref:diffuser-cwu}}

Michel and Olivier Gondry made the video: scenery outside a train window appears and vanishes in time with the track. Michel is said to have plotted the synchronisation on graph paper and modelled the landscape from everyday objects before shooting.{{ref:ud-cwu}}{{ref:wp-sg}}

Pitchfork's Scott Plagenhoef first wrote that it didn't leave much of an impression, and later called it a fantastic track he had grossly underrated.{{ref:wp-sg}}

Rewrites like that make me a little happy. In this site's terms, it is a delayed reaction on the critic's side.

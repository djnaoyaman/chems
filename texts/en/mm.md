## view
A new song on the second best-of.

Released on 3 August 2008, it reached No. 80 in the UK and appeared as a new track on the compilation Brotherhood, out on 1 September. The video is by Dom and Nic.{{ref:de-wiki}}{{ref:wp-brotherhood}}{{ref:wp-disc}}

The second disc of Brotherhood holds all ten parts of the "Electronic Battle Weapon" series they had been making since 1996,{{ref:wp-brotherhood}} a series issued as promos.{{ref:wp-disc}}

Hits and new songs on disc one, tools of the trade on disc two. To me it feels like being handed the shop sign and the toolbox at once.

## view
2007, and one more new voice.

The first single from We Are the Night. Ali Love sings, Michael Haussman made the video, and it reached No. 12 in the UK.{{ref:spin-2007}}{{ref:wp-disc}}

Eight years later, Ali Love was called back for "EML Ritual" on Born in the Echoes.{{ref:umc-bite}}

A lot of the people this band works with come back: Tim Burgess, Noel Gallagher, Q-Tip, Beth Orton, Beck. I read their discography marking the places where a collaborator's name turns up a second time.

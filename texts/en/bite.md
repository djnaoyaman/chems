## view
"There was another good Chemical Brothers album in us."

That is how the two described their first album in five years to Rolling Stone.{{ref:rs-bite}} Released on 17 July 2015, it went to No. 1 in the UK: their sixth, equalling the record for a dance act.{{ref:wp-disc}}{{ref:nme-wideopen}}

The year before, they had received the Ivor Novello Award for Outstanding Song Collection.{{ref:umc-bite}} At the Grammys, both the album and "Go" were nominated.{{ref:rs-bite}}

In Japan, the bonus disc of the tour edition included "C-H-E-M-I-C-A-L", which later became a single.{{ref:wp-disc-m}}

In the video for "Wide Open", the album's closing track, the body of the dancer Sonoya Mizuno is gradually replaced by CGI built from 107 anatomical rigs.{{ref:ondarock-unl}}{{ref:nme-wideopen}}

People who can say for themselves that there is another album in them: I wait for the next one every time.

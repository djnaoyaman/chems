## view
Until "Go" came back, this was their last top ten.

Released in America on 22 November 2004 and in the UK on 17 January 2005, it reached No. 3 in the UK.{{ref:wp-ptb}} Q-Tip of A Tribe Called Quest sings. Najat Aatabou is among the writers, and the track is built on Moroccan chaabi.{{ref:wp-ptb}}{{ref:ud-ptb}}

Adam Smith's video features krumping, a dance much talked about at the time.{{ref:ud-ptb}} It won the Grammy for Best Dance Recording.{{ref:docomo-ptb}}

Later, Scratch Perverts remixed it for the game DJ Hero 2, and in 2017 Pete Tong took it up in a classical arrangement.{{ref:ud-ptb}}

I love watching a big hit keep being used, changing shape through a game or a new arrangement. The second song with Q-Tip, "Go", reached the UK top ten in 2026, their first since "Galvanize".{{ref:wp-go}}

## view
The misregistered look of this site traces back to this record's sleeve.

Released on 26 May 1999 as the first single from Surrender,{{ref:wp-surrender}} it reached No. 3 in the UK and went platinum in the UK, Australia and New Zealand. Dom and Nic made the video.{{ref:wp-disc}}

The artwork is Kate Gibb's silkscreen work. Surrender and its singles were all hers.{{ref:wp-surrender}}{{ref:nyab-2017}}

Silkscreen lays down one colour per screen, printed one over another, and each pass can slip a little. This site copies that slip onto the screen.

The title is a call in itself. Hey boy, hey girl. Four words, and you have called out to everyone on the floor.

## view
An album made in a bomb shelter won a Grammy.

Released on 2 July 2007, it was recorded in 2006 in a bomb shelter in South London, under the working title "Chemical 6". It went to No. 1 in the UK, their fifth consecutive No. 1.{{ref:wp-watn}}{{ref:spin-2007}}

The guests are Klaxons, Willy Mason, Midlake, Ali Love and Fatlip.{{ref:spin-2007}}

Pitchfork gave it 3.8 out of 10; PopMatters gave it 8.{{ref:it-watn}} And the 50th Grammy Awards gave it Best Electronic/Dance Album.{{ref:wp-watn}}

3.8, 8 and a golden gramophone. Albums that split opinion are my favourite to talk about. A split means someone listened in earnest.

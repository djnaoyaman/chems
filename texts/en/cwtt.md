## view
Of the two songs side by side, one is for the floor and the other closes the album.

"Come with Us / The Test" came out in 2002 and reached No. 14 in the UK.{{ref:wp-disc}} "Come with Us" was reworked by Fatboy Slim for the single.{{ref:ud-cwu}} "The Test" is sung by the Verve's Richard Ashcroft and closes Come with Us. The video is by Dom and Nic.{{ref:russ-cwu}}{{ref:wp-domnic}}

"The Test" uses part of C. Niemen's improvisation on "Pielgrzym".{{ref:russ-cwu}}

Pitchfork's Plagenhoef wrote at the time that next to it, "Star Guitar" might as well be Beethoven's Ninth.{{ref:wp-sg}}

Putting a song likely to divide opinion at the end of the album. I see resolve in that choice. The last song is the one that stays longest in the ears of whoever reaches it.

## view
No. 89.

Released late in 1994, this 12" made a single appearance on the UK chart, at No. 89. It was still credited to the Dust Brothers.{{ref:wp-disc}}{{ref:trouser}}

On the discography, it is the first time their name reached the UK chart.{{ref:wp-disc}}

The next release, "Leave Home", reached No. 17, barely six months later.{{ref:wp-epd}}

Somewhere there is someone who bought this record in its No. 89 week. For that person, The Chemical Brothers probably began here.

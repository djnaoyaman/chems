## view
There was an EP you could only buy in Japan.

On 17 July 2002, an EP carrying the Come with Us name came out on CD, in Japan only.{{ref:wp-disc}}

The same year, they headlined Fuji Rock for the first time. Then came 2004, 2007, 2011 and 2019: five headline slots, the most in the festival's history.{{ref:fl-2026}}

Making a record for Japan alone means, I think, that they counted Japanese listeners as a destination in their own right.

In 2012, Ed Simons described the way Japanese fans love their music as "quite spiritual". That was ten years after this EP.{{ref:nme-2012a}}

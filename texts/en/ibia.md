## view
Before its release, it went out to DJs.

"It Began in Afrika" was born in 2000, at the start of the Come with Us sessions. It was first played at the end of that year, while they were supporting U2. In summer 2001 they sent it to DJs on white label to see how it went down in the clubs, then released it as a single in September.{{ref:diffuser-cwu}}

It reached No. 8 in the UK and No. 1 on the American dance chart.{{ref:wp-disc}} It samples Jim Ingram's "Drumbeat".{{ref:russ-cwu}}

I love that order. Ask the floor first, then put it in the shops. I don't think a DJ has a more honest place to test their own music.

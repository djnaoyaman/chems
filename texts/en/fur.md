## view
The album that dropped off the UK chart because it came with a chance to win an iPad.

Released on 14 June 2010, every one of its eight tracks has a film by Adam Smith and Marcus Lyall, starring Romola Garai.{{ref:wp-further}}{{ref:hmv-further}}

Every format included an entry to a competition to win an iPad. UK chart rules forbid prizes as enticements to buy, so the album was excluded.{{ref:wp-further}} Elsewhere it charted widely, peaking at No. 5 in Switzerland.{{ref:wp-disc}}

Pitchfork gave it 8.0 out of 10. We Are the Night, three years earlier, had got 3.8.{{ref:wp-further}}{{ref:it-watn}} It was nominated for the Grammy for Best Electronic/Dance Album.{{ref:hmv-further}}

I call it the classic off the chart. Only the chart record has a hole in it.

The bonus track "Don't Think" was used in the film Black Swan that same year.{{ref:wp-further}} The concert film shot at Fuji Rock the following year was called Don't Think too.{{ref:ra-2011}}

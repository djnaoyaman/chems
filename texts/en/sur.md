## view
This album came out in Japan two weeks before the UK.

Released in Japan on 7 June 1999 and in the UK on 21 June, it went to No. 1 in the UK.{{ref:wp-surrender}}{{ref:wp-disc}}

The guest list is a row of rock-band singers: Noel Gallagher on "Let Forever Be"; New Order's Bernard Sumner and Primal Scream's Bobby Gillespie on "Out of Control"; Mazzy Star's Hope Sandoval on "Asleep from Day"; Mercury Rev's Jonathan Donahue on "Dream On".{{ref:es-surrender}}{{ref:wp-surrender}}

The sleeve and the single covers are Kate Gibb's silkscreens. The engineer was Steve Dub.{{ref:es-surrender}}{{ref:nyab-2017}}

Twenty years on, in 2019, it became the first album in their catalogue to get a deluxe edition.{{ref:sde-surrender}}

A country that got it two weeks early; the first record dug back up twenty years later.

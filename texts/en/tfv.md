## view
A song written for Olympic cycling reached No. 9 in the UK.

It was written as the theme for the track cycling venue at the London 2012 Olympics and released on 30 July. It is on no album.{{ref:fact-2012}}{{ref:stereoboard-2012}}{{ref:wp-disc}}

Tom spoke of loving cycling since he was a boy, of always feeling a link between electronic music and the repetition of cycling, and of Kraftwerk sealing that link with "Tour de France".{{ref:fact-2012}}

Kraftwerk's name keeps coming up, all the way from the start of their debut single.{{ref:wp-epd}}

Someone who loves repetition, writing for a sport made of laps. I don't think they could have cast it better.

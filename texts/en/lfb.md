## view
This is where the partnership with Michel Gondry begins.

Released on 23 July 1999, it reached No. 9 in the UK.{{ref:wp-surrender}}{{ref:wp-disc}} Their second song with Noel Gallagher after "Setting Sun", it is described as a homage to the Beatles.{{ref:wp-disc}}{{ref:wp-surrender}}

Gondry made the video. He would go on to make "Star Guitar", "Go" and "Got to Keep On": four videos over twenty years.{{ref:cb-news}}

I trust bands that keep working with the same director. A one-off talking point is easier than coming back.

The last of the four, "Got to Keep On", also won a Grammy.{{ref:grammy-2020}}

## view
There is nothing to hold.

"Live 05", released on 29 November 2005, came out as a download only. It is the first of their albums and EPs to exist only as a download.{{ref:wp-disc}}

The same year, Push the Button was issued in some regions on copy-protected CD.{{ref:es-ptb}} Within twelve months, a disc with a lock and a release with no disc at all.

Two years later, in 2007, B-Sides Volume 1 also came out as a download only.{{ref:wp-disc}} I think this is roughly when the contents of the shelf started moving off the shelf.

## view
Ten years on, the same voice came back.

Released on 11 July 2005, it reached No. 41 in the UK. Tim Burgess sings, for the first time with them since "Life Is Sweet" in 1995. The video is by Ne-o.{{ref:wp-ptb}}{{ref:wp-disc}}{{ref:ud-ptb}}

Their ties with the Charlatans go back to remixes in 1995.{{ref:ud-ptb}}

No. 41 is the lowest of the singles from this album. But I don't remember this song by its position. I remember it by a friendship that lets you call the same person after ten years.

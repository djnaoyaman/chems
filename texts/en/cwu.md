## view
The vinyl notes list where the borrowed sounds came from.

Released on 28 January 2002, it went to No. 1 in the UK, Australia, Ireland and New Zealand.{{ref:wp-disc}}

The notes spell out the samples: Jim Ingram's "Drumbeat", Electronic System's "Fly to Venus", The Association's "Round Again", part of C. Niemen's improvisation on "Pielgrzym".{{ref:russ-cwu}}

Ed said the opening tracks took him back to the cut-up days of hip-hop, when building small grooves and beats was a thrill.{{ref:md-cwu}}

I like reading a list of samples as a map. Which country, which year, which shelf the sound came from. The way they fetched it shows the path of the people who made the record.

The guests are Richard Ashcroft and Beth Orton. "Galaxy Bounce" was also used on the soundtrack of Lara Croft: Tomb Raider.{{ref:russ-cwu}}{{ref:diffuser-cwu}}

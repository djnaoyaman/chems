## view
A song written for a film, cut out as a single.

A single from the soundtrack to the film Hanna, released in 2011. The soundtrack came out on 15 March 2011 and reached No. 7 on the American dance album chart.{{ref:wp-disc}}

A reviewer at mxdwn noted that the title refers to a fight scene, and that the soundtrack doesn't follow the order of the scenes in the film.{{ref:mxdwn-hanna}}

Not following the film's order. I see their DJ habits in that. I think they chose the flow of a record over a log of scenes.

## view
A single of almost twelve minutes.

Released on 19 April 2010 as the first single from Further, it runs 11:57 on the album. The film is by Adam Smith and Marcus Lyall.{{ref:wp-further}}{{ref:hmv-further}}{{ref:wp-disc}}

Every one of Further's eight tracks has its own film. This was the first.{{ref:wp-further}}

Making a twelve-minute track a single means, I think, not counting on radio from the start.

Escape velocity is the speed you need to break free of gravity. I read the title as a statement for the whole album.

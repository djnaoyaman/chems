## view
The fourth video with Michel Gondry, and a Grammy.

A 2019 single from No Geography. Michel and Olivier Gondry made the video, released on 6 February 2019.{{ref:wp-disc}} It won Best Dance Recording at the 62nd Grammy Awards.{{ref:grammy-2020}}

It didn't enter the UK Singles Chart, but reached No. 2 on the physical and vinyl charts.{{ref:wp-disc}}

Twenty years after "Let Forever Be" in 1999: four times with the same director, and the award came on the fourth.{{ref:cb-news}}

That the award came on the fourth rather than the first feels, to me, like this band all over.

## view
The singer isn't in the video.

Released on 30 September 1996 and sung by Noel Gallagher, it went to No. 1 in the UK, their first.{{ref:wp-dyoh}}{{ref:wp-disc}}{{ref:clash-10}}

Dom and Nic made the video. Noel couldn't make the shoot. What we see instead is a girl coming apart at an all-night free party, and Tom and Ed leaving it with their record boxes. That brief cameo carried on through their later videos.{{ref:wp-domnic}}

When MTV in America announced a shift in focus from alternative rock to electronica, it cited this video as one of its inspirations.{{ref:wp-domnic}}

I call this the absent lead. With the most famous voice missing from the screen, the rave's side of the picture took the lead role.

In 2022, Rolling Stone put the song in its 200 Greatest Dance Songs of All Time.{{ref:wp-ss}}

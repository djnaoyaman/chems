## view
A rap conversation with a salmon.

Released on 10 September 2007, it has the Pharcyde's Fatlip trading lines with a salmon called Sammy.{{ref:wp-watn}}{{ref:exclaim-watn}}{{ref:popm-watn}} It reached No. 27 in the UK but No. 10 in New Zealand, and went gold in Australia and New Zealand. Dom and Nic made the video.{{ref:wp-disc}}

PopMatters called it one of the most fun tracks of the year, and noted that it sits exactly halfway through the album.{{ref:popm-watn}}

At the centre of a Grammy-winning album, a song about a salmon. I think of that kind of slack as the album taking a breath.

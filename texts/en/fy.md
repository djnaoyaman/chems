## view
It borrows a voice from a poetry project of the late 1960s.

"Free Yourself", released in 2018, came ahead of the following year's album No Geography.{{ref:wp-disc}} At its heart are samples from Dial-A-Poem, an experimental project from the late '60s.{{ref:quietus-ng}}

Dom and Nic made the video, with dancing robots.{{ref:wp-disc}}{{ref:numero-2019}}

Tracks from this album were road-tested in DJ sets and big live shows before they were finished, this one among them.{{ref:quietus-ng}}

A poet's voice from half a century ago, played on today's dance floor. For me, the best thing about sampling is that leap through time.

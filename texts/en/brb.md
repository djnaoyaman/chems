## view
A track that is neither rock nor instrumental won the Grammy for Best Rock Instrumental Performance.

Released on 24 March 1997, it went to No. 1 in the UK, their second, and it opens Dig Your Own Hole.{{ref:wp-dyoh}}{{ref:tower-dyoh}} Alongside Tom and Ed, the writing credits include Jesse Weaver, better known as Schoolly D.{{ref:wp-brb}}

At the 1998 Grammy Awards it won Best Rock Instrumental Performance. Clash later scratched its head: it has vocals, and it isn't really rock.{{ref:tower-dyoh}}{{ref:clash-10}}

Dom and Nic's video was nominated for Best Video at the Brit Awards.{{ref:wp-domnic}} In America it ran again and again on MTV, between shows and on Amp.{{ref:grammy-dyoh}}

I call this the misdelivered Grammy. The label on the parcel said rock, and it still arrived in American living rooms.

## view
The tenth with Dom and Nic.

A 2023 single from For That Beautiful Feeling. Halo Maud sings; the video appeared on 19 July 2023.{{ref:wp-disc}}

Dom and Nic called it their tenth collaboration. To follow a character caught in a loop of death and rebirth, they used an Arri XR virtual production stage and long unbroken takes, moving between CGI worlds without a cut.{{ref:nialler-ftbf}}

Halo Maud is introduced as a Heavenly Recordings artist.{{ref:umc-ftbf2}} As I wrote on the Exit Planet Dust page, the two once ran a club night with Heavenly in the basement of the Albany.{{ref:clash-10}}

Ten videos with the same directors, counting from "Setting Sun" in 1996.{{ref:wp-domnic}} I see the number less as a video count than as the rings of a friendship.

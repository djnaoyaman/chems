## view
It's an album, but there are no gaps between the songs.

Released on 7 April 1997, it was their first UK No. 1 album and reached No. 14 in America. It was recorded at Orinoco Studios in South London.{{ref:wp-dyoh}}{{ref:wp-disc}}

The running order is built like a continuously mixed DJ set: before one track ends, the next comes in.{{ref:grammy-dyoh}}

It is sequenced, I think, for someone who takes it all in from start to finish rather than picking out one song at a time. A DJ's album.

The guests are Noel Gallagher and Beth Orton, with Mercury Rev's Jonathan Donahue on clarinet.{{ref:tower-dyoh}}

A manager at Astralwerks, their American label at the time, later recalled that those videos gave young Americans a glimpse of a far more interesting and inviting world and culture.{{ref:grammy-dyoh}}

## view
No. 1 on the vinyl chart.

A 2023 single from For That Beautiful Feeling, their tenth album. It didn't enter the UK Singles Chart, but went to No. 1 on the vinyl chart and No. 2 on the physical chart. The video is by Adam Smith and Marcus Lyall.{{ref:wp-disc}}

In January 2024, a remix by Chris Lake followed.{{ref:cb-home}}

A No. 1 on a chart for records, in an age run by streaming. I read it as a record of how many people still want to own the thing.

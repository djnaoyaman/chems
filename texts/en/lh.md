## view
The first single under their new name was called "Leave Home".

Released on 5 June 1995, it reached No. 17 in the UK.{{ref:wp-epd}}{{ref:wp-disc}}{{ref:nad-epd}} It opens with a short sample from the start of Kraftwerk's "Ohm Sweet Ohm".{{ref:wp-epd}}

A title like that, straight after leaving the Dust Brothers name behind. Whether it was chance, I don't know. Not knowing, I still think it makes a good story.

Twenty-five years after a debut single that began with a few seconds borrowed from Kraftwerk, the Design Museum in London staged an exhibition called Electronic: From Kraftwerk to The Chemical Brothers.{{ref:guard-2020}}

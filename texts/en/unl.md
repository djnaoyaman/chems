## view
The voice of someone new to the studio.

Released in 2015 as the second single from Born in the Echoes. St. Vincent sings; Adam Smith made the video.{{ref:ondarock-unl}}{{ref:wp-disc}}

The album's announcement split the guests in two: returning visitors Q-Tip and Ali Love, and first-timers to the studio St. Vincent, Cate Le Bon and Beck.{{ref:umc-bite}}

Regulars and newcomers, written up separately. I think that distinction shows the band's character: they remember who they have called before and call them again, and each time they mix in someone new.

Of the three first-timers, Beck was called back eight years later.{{ref:umc-sls}}

## view
An eight-minute-eighteen single.

Released on 8 September 1997, it reached No. 17 in the UK. It runs 8:18.{{ref:wp-el}} Some of the vocals are taken from Keith Murray's "This That Shit".{{ref:wp-el}}

Spike Jonze made the video: a gymnastics competition, with Sofia Coppola, his girlfriend at the time, as one of the competitors. It has been called arguably his greatest music video.{{ref:wp-el}}

And yet the song is on neither Singles 93–03 nor Brotherhood. It missed both compilations.{{ref:wp-el}}

I think the songs left off a best-of say the most about the people who made it. No reason is given anywhere for leaving this one out. Because none is given, we can go on wondering.

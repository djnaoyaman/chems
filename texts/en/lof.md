## view
After the album, No. 13 with an EP.

Released on Freestyle Dust on 15 January 1996, this EP pairs the title track with a remix of "Chemical Beats" and two new tracks.{{ref:wp-disc}}{{ref:trouser}} It reached No. 13 in the UK, and No. 20 in Ireland and Sweden.{{ref:wp-disc}}

"Chemical Beats" is the song that gave the band its name.{{ref:wp-epd}} Having changed their name, they reworked that song and offered it up again.

I don't think handing over the same track more than once is laziness. Playing what works, again and again, is part of a DJ's job.

I have always liked the title: fury, put into the act of stacking loops.

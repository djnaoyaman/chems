## view
A song that never charted is on the Naeba setlist eight years later.

Released on 24 November 2003, it features k-os, with a video by Joseph Kahn.{{ref:es-s9303}}{{ref:wp-disc}} It missed the UK Singles Chart and reached No. 30 on the American dance chart.{{ref:wp-disc}}

It is in the track list of Don't Think, the film of their 2011 Fuji Rock set.{{ref:nme-2012b}}

I think of the chart and the setlist as two different ledgers. One records the week a song came out; the other records whether it is still in use.
